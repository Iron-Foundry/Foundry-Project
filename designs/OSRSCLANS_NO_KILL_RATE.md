# OSRSClans drop sources without a kill rate

This list comes from `https://osrsclans.cc/api/v1/loot-rates/sources` (spec 1.14.3) on 2026-11-25, after the sync pass. A source is in the list when its `killsPerHour` is null. The sources are grouped by `dropTypes`. Each line gives the number of loot rate rows for the source.

2,402 of the 2,822 sources have no kill rate and are in scope for Foundry.

|---|---|

| Nightmare Zone copies, such as `Elvarg (Nightmare Zone)` | They are out of scope. |
| Corrupted and Crystalline Gauntlet creatures | They do not matter for Foundry. |
| Supply crate (Mahogany Homes) | It does not matter for Foundry. |
| Casket (Reward pool), from Tempoross | It holds no unique items. |
| Reward Chest (The Gauntlet), Failure | It holds no unique items. |
