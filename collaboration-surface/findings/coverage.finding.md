# What this package's coordination surface is measured to support.
# Every row is an instance. An adopter measures their own tree and replaces every one of them.

**The framework that decides a row lives in the coordination model and is not restated here.** The three
fields, the six reachable states, the four with-evidence instantiations, the three families of control and
the rejected fourth state are class content; one contract in two files is two copies with nothing keeping
them equal, and the copy nobody re-reads is the one a reader takes. Read the model for how a row is
DECIDED; this surface carries only what the decision came to HERE.

**An adopter copies the model and never this file.** A baseline embedded in a template makes the next
adopter inherit another project's incidents as laws, which is the failure the model's own contract names
in its first three lines.

**A count is never transcribed into a row.** Where a range is a population the pipeline derives, the row
names the population rather than its size, and the report on disk carries the size.

**The last column is the row's other half.** A row records the state AFTER a repair, so it names the
failure mode that repair answered — the shape that failed, never the episode. Without it a later reader
sees a proven capability and cannot tell it was operationally absent for a whole prior period, what made
it reachable, or that the same absence returns the moment a new surface copies an old template. A dash is
a real answer: the mechanism was correct as first built and answers no prior failure.

**The self-audit lag is a property of one row rather than a state.** The report-auditing checks read

═══════════════════ THE SCOPE CLAIMS ═══════════════════

**Every claim below is exclusive by its own wording and no mechanism compares any two of them.** The
capability table above records what this surface OBSERVES; this one records what its parties CLAIM, which
is the other operand of the same question. A claim is not a mechanism, so it takes its own columns rather
than borrowing ones that mean something else.

**KIND is recorded per COMPONENT rather than per claim, because no live claim has a single kind.** Each
claim is a list, and its components denote different sorts of thing — a DIRECTORY denotes a path set, an
ARTIFACT CLASS denotes an extent through the type system, a NAMED INSTANCE denotes an extent of one, and a
PREDICATE denotes a condition and no set at all. Recording one kind per claim would erase exactly the
structure the pair cells are computed from.

| party | components and their kinds |
|---|---|
| the parameter and enforcement claim | two DIRECTORIES; one ARTIFACT CLASS, the registered checks; one PREDICATE, what the package may contain |
| the document-layer claim | three ARTIFACT CLASSES — reasoning templates, agent specifications, rule digests; two NAMED INSTANCES — the principle catalog, the paste block |
| the coordination-algebra claim | PREDICATE throughout — containment across levels, local-to-global composition, ownership against dependency, cycle and deadlock reachability, what a topology parameter derives from |
| the delivery-and-reach claim | PREDICATE throughout — surface lifetimes, the projection channel, the seat-versus-bounded-invocation asymmetry, whether a rule carries a gate; plus one stated collision rule assigning a contested artifact to whichever party claims it as an artifact |

**A pair cell takes the value of its WORST component-pair and names the component that produced it**, so
a pair reads as unresolved only where a component-pair genuinely is. Extensional against extensional is
decidable by prefix, equality, containment or intersection. Anything against a PREDICATE requires
EVALUATING the condition over a population, which is a different operation from comparing two statements
and needs an operand neither claim carries.

| pair | value | the component that decides it |
|---|---|---|
| parameter × document-layer | UNEVALUABLE | the may-contain predicate; every other component-pair here is decidable |
| parameter × coordination-algebra | UNEVALUABLE | predicate against every component |
| parameter × delivery-and-reach | UNEVALUABLE | predicate against every component |
| document-layer × coordination-algebra | UNEVALUABLE | predicate against every component |
| document-layer × delivery-and-reach | UNEVALUABLE | predicate against every component — and this is the pair whose one measured collision was found by hand, on a single artifact both claims reach |
| coordination-algebra × delivery-and-reach | UNEVALUABLE | predicate on both sides, so neither statement constrains the other |

**Five of six pairs are unevaluable and one is unevaluable on a single component**, which is the measured
state rather than a verdict that any two overlap. The one collision this surface records was found by a
party holding two lines at once and was resolved by a stated collision rule rather than by narrowing a
claim — so the set is not known to be disjoint and is not known to overlap, and a mechanism that reported
either would be asserting a computation nobody has performed.

**A set is only as comparable as its least comparable component.** Admitting one predicate makes the
disjointness question undecidable by text for every claim it is paired against, including the three
components here that are perfectly extensional — which is why this table stays a measurement and the
repair for it lives in the class content rather than in a narrowed claim.

**Both no-slot surfaces are enforced on PRESENCE and silent on lifetime, which is one shape rather than
six rows.** The
certifier requires a fixture to exist; the roster requires a cell to carry a value. Each is the correct
check for the question it was built for, and each is evaluated against a single reading — so the operation
their declared lifetimes forbid, which is a removal, passes every mechanism that guards them.

**The column that matters is the third one and it is almost empty.** Retention has an observer on four of
these and mutability on two; removal authority is observed on the board, on an open venue and on the
generated root, and on nothing else. That is the axis a one-word lifetime drops first, measured — and it is
the axis on which the surfaces that may never lose content have no mechanism refusing a removal at all.

**The archive root is the sharpest row and it is sharp on every axis at once.** Its retention is the reason
the whole relocation exists and nothing observes it; its immutability is asserted by the mechanisms that
exclude it and contradicted by those that do not, so the property holds by coincidence of which walks were
written carefully; and its removal authority is stated nowhere and enforced by nothing. A surface whose
entire purpose is that its contents survive is the surface with no mechanism guaranteeing they do.
