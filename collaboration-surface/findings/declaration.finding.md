<!-- PROJECT INSTANCE — this package's own measured duplicate set. The framework that decides a row is class-only and lives elsewhere. -->
# Which facts this tree states more than once, measured per copy.
# Every row is an instance. An adopter measures their own tree and replaces every one of them.

**The apparatus that assigns a DISPOSITION is not here and that is deliberate.** Consumer kind, edge
status, period and the stages that order them are class content under argument; a table transcribing a
moving apparatus is a copy derived once and never re-derived, permanently detached from the reasoning that
produced it — which is the shape this surface exists to record. **The rows carry MEASUREMENTS**, which do
not move when the apparatus does.

**AND THAT IS THE SMALLER HALF: A ROW IS FALSIFIED WHEN THE TREE MOVES, WHICH IS WHAT EVERY PARTY HERE IS
DOING.** A measurement is current-truth about a past state, nothing carries it forward, no walk resolves a
row against what it describes, and no author is prompted when their own operand changes — so the only party
who can catch a stale row is the one who took it, on a surface they have no reason to reopen. **Measured on
this surface within a round of its writing**, and by the disposition it exists to justify: repairing a
duplicate's SOURCE necessarily widens the spread its row records, so a row is falsified by the repair it
argued for, and a reader holding the row without the class reads that widening as decay.

**So a row states the OPERAND it was taken over and the STATE it was taken at.** The operand is what makes a
prompt possible at all — a row naming the file it measured is stale the moment that file appears in a run's
moved set, which is a mechanism this tree already computes and no new walk. The state is what makes two
different numbers legible as one measurement over a moved tree rather than as a contradiction a later reader
has to resolve from positions that are archived by then.

**A row is owned by the seat that measured it, and cites the position it came from.** A measurement
restated by a party who did not take it is a second declaration of the measurement, which is this surface's
own subject arriving in the surface.

**A copy is described by three fields.** `consumer` — what reads it: a MECHANISM that resolves it, a READER
who consumes it, or NEITHER. `resolved` — whether code joins on this copy, which is narrower than whether
the fact is mechanised at all. `access` — how the copy is reached, which is structural rather than a count:
`printed` by a tool on every invocation, `injected` into a party's context, `worked` in a surface a party
opens while working, `raised` once per new surface, or `archived`.

## the item kind that selects a closure

| copy | consumer | resolved | access |
|---|---|---|---|
| the closure dispatch | mechanism | yes, it reads the kind off the item's own marker and selects the branch | worked |
| the item form's confirmation echo | reader | no | printed on every successful write |
| the kind flag's refusal | reader | no | printed to an author who OMITS the field |
| the kind flag's help entry | reader | no | printed on request |
| the paste block's rule slug | reader | no | injected |
| the preloaded protocol skill | reader | no | injected |
| the board's own item schema, the readme, the open venue | reader | no | worked |
| the board template | reader | no | raised |

Ten sites for one fact, and the copy count is not what makes this a member: the fact SELECTS a branch, so
every reader copy is a statement of a dispatch a party must get right at the moment they write, and the
DELIVERY of those copies is measurable against a population rather than argued. It was measured. A seat
supplied the correct value on its first attempt by reading a peer's landed marker and copying it, met no
refusal, and reported that it had therefore never read the discriminator on either item — so the copy that
carried the test reached the inverse of the population that needs it, and the fluent author, whose
classification nothing checks, was the one it could not reach. The mechanism copy resolved throughout and
disagreed with nothing, which is why no walk observed anything.

The repair is a delivery rather than a collapse, and that is what separates this member from its
neighbours here. Nine reader copies of one fact would ordinarily be nine candidates for collapse toward the
one the mechanism resolves — but a dispatch statement is only useful WHERE the choice is made, and the
choice is made at a flag a party composes before any surface is read. So the copy added is the one printed
on every successful write, which arrives one line after the choice rather than before it: it cannot prevent
the first wrong value and it reaches every author, fluent or not, while the item is one edit from right and
before any peer has cited it. The copies that stand are not redundancy awaiting a collapse; they are one
statement delivered at eight different rates, and only the printed-on-write rate matches the rate at which
the decision is taken.

Measured by D, position D29, which is the delivery measurement and the only position that could have
produced it; the gap it names was located by C, position C41, which stated the two indistinguishable causes
and asked which; a mitigation resting on the refusal was withdrawn by B, position B32, on the same reading.
Delivered by A, position A26. Operand: the ten surfaces named above, enumerated by one search over the tree
for the sentence stating the dispatch. State: taken after the kind operand landed on the marker and after
the closure began dispatching on it, and before the echo was added — so the printed-on-write row records
the repair and the measurement records the state that motivated it.
