web-app React 19 + TanStack Router + Shadcn. 6 pages, 1 shell, 1 pinned bar.

Type: shell

MusicPanel

One dialog, a grouped nav, one page at a time - built like the Control Panel. The nav collapses into a Sheet below md.

src/components/music/MusicPanel.tsx

MiniPlayer

Pinned to the bottom of every page while something plays, hidden while the panel is open. A status line first, a control second.

src/components/music/MiniPlayer.tsx

Type: pages

Now Playing

Cover, requester, source path, seek bar with a scrub readout, transport, and the next five queued.

src/components/music/NowPlayingPage.tsx

Queue

Search to add, drag to reorder, jump, remove, and save the whole thing as a playlist.

src/components/music/QueuePage.tsx

Playlists

Import, create, rename, share, delete. Opening one reveals its tracks: reorder, remove, queue one, queue all.

src/components/music/PlaylistsPage.tsx · PlaylistRow.tsx · PlaylistTracks.tsx

History

Everything the session has played, newest first, with requeue and save-to-playlist. The panel shows ten; this shows all of it.

src/components/music/HistoryPage.tsx

Clan Stats

Totals over a chosen window, where the audio actually streamed from, and the most-played recordings.

src/components/music/StatsPage.tsx

Activity

Who did what to this session, newest first, capped at 25 and gone when the session ends.

src/components/music/ActivityPage.tsx

Type: controls

TrackSearch

Shared by the queue and the playlist editor. Searching needs no session - it is a Lavalink lookup, not playback.

src/components/music/TrackSearch.tsx

TransportControls · TrackArt

The transport row, and the cover-or-placeholder used by every list.

src/components/music/TransportControls.tsx · TrackArt.tsx

Type: dialogs and prompts

AddToPlaylistDialog · delete confirmations

Saving a played track into a library playlist, and the two destructive prompts.

src/components/music/AddToPlaylistDialog.tsx · PlaylistRow.tsx · PlaylistTracks.tsx

Type: transport

useMusicSocket

One socket for the whole page, shared by the panel and the mini player.

src/hooks/useMusicSocket.ts

discord-utils Components V2 panel, five ephemeral views, four slash commands.

Type: the panel

MusicPanel (LayoutView)

One components-v2 message per session, edited in place. The accent colour is the source the audio actually came from.

music/views/panel_view.py · format.py

Type: control rows

transport_row · modifier_row · views_row

Three rows, capped at five buttons each. Every callback runs the in-channel check before it touches playback.

music/views/controls.py · buttons.py

Type: ephemeral views

History view

The last ten played, each with a numbered button that queues it again. Ten is what fits in two rows of five.

music/views/history_view.py

Queue view · Playlist picker · Search picker · Seek modal

The remaining ephemeral surfaces, all reached from views_row or a command.

music/views/queue_view.py · playlist_view.py · picker.py · modals.py

Type: core paths

enqueue · _play

The funnel every surface reaches, and the moment a cover can be recovered.

music/session.py · naming.py

api-backend Nine routers, four services. The only place the browser's view is shaped.

Type: routers and payloads

GET /music/sessions/{id}

The live session as the website renders it. Audio handles never appear.

app/routers/music/sessions.py · _live_schemas.py

POST /music/sessions/{id}/commands

One body for every action. 200 means published, not performed.

app/routers/music/control.py · _command_schemas.py

Playlists · import · stats · bot surface

The durable half, plus the read-only surface discord-utils uses.

app/routers/music/playlists.py · importing.py · stats.py · bot.py

Type: services

MusicStatsService

The consumer that turns the event stream into durable counters.

app/services/music_stats.py · music_stream.py · music_counters.py
