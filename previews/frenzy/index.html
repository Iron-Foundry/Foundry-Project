Frenzy overhaul

Three directions for the public event page, the public team page and the staff page, in the picked Monogram skin. F1 is tables first, F2 is visual progress, F3 is one page per subject. Pick one direction per surface, or one for all three. The items, drop chances, kill rates and item images are live OSRSClans rows from 25 Nov 2026, each from the fastest source. The teams, points, players, goals and submissions are mock. Dark frames show a signed-in staff member. On the public pages, the light frames show a signed-out visitor. Today's row of each set sits at the bottom.
