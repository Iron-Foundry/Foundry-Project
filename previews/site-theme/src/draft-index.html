Frenzy draft (FRZ-3)

Round 3 (the first three tabs) joins the snake grid, a recent-picks list and every team's full roster on one page. D4 Stacked puts the grid beside the recent picks, then the pool, then the teams. D5 Grid first runs the grid across the page, then the pool beside a longer recent-picks list, then the teams. Each team column lists its captain and all its members, top picks numbered and handed-out members marked auto, and scrolls inside its column. The third tab is the finished draft in this layout. The tabs after it are round 2.

Round 2, at real size: 160 signups, 4 captains, teams of 40, in Top Picks mode with 5 top picks each. Three candidates for the live page: D1 Board shows each team's count and latest picks, D2 Pick feed runs the top picks as a numbered list beside the pool, D3 Snake grid draws the 5 top-pick rounds as rows and folds the handed-out rounds into one line. The other tabs are shared: the drawn order, the result after the snake hands out the rest, a team's full roster with Move to, Full draft mode at pick 87 (staff view, "Pick for (captain)"), and the staff setup. In the live tabs the viewer is Hammer Time on their own turn, so the buttons read "Pick". Long lists scroll in their own frame here; the product draws them as virtual lists. Names and figures are mock.
