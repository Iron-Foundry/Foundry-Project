Clan Rules

By joining Iron Foundry you agree to abide by the following rules.

  1. 1

    Follow all Jagex rules, or don't, but don't broadcast what you are doing if that is the case.

  2. 2

    Racist, sexist, homophobic, discriminatory and all other remarks that may be considered hostile to other people will not be tolerated.

  3. 3

    Topics such as politics, religion, etc, can only be discussed publicly if it is by means of constructive conversation. Otherwise, it should be taken to DMs.

  4. 4

    Scamming, luring, begging or advertising services is not allowed.

  5. 5

    Unless mutually agreed upon beforehand, you may not PK other clan members.

  6. 6

    Unless mutually agreed upon beforehand, all PvM drops are regarded as FFA.
