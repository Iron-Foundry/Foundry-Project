Site theme - members area (RDM-2 batch 2)

The picked Monogram skin on all members pages. Two candidates for how you move between them: M1 puts the pages in the row under the bar, the same as the public sections. M2 keeps a rail beside the content, as today. Both carry the same pages. Light mode and today follow. Names, figures, tickets and answers are mock.

M1 Page row

The members pages sit in the page row under the bar, the same row the public sections use. Settings sits at the right end of the row. The content takes the full width.

M2 Side rail

The members pages sit in a rail beside the content, with your name at its head and Settings at its foot, as today. Under 900 px the rail becomes the page row.

M1 Page row, light mode

M1 in the light mode that the Settings page switches to. M2 takes the same tokens.
