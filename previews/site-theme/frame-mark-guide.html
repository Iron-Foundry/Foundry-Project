Chambers of Xeric: learner guide

Ferrous FenUpdated 14 Nov 202638

On this page
  • Requirements
  • Gear
  • Room order

This guide takes a new raider from the first scout to a clean completion. It covers the stats we ask for on clan learner runs, a budget gear setup, and the order we take the rooms in.

Requirements

StatLearner runComfortable
Attack / Strength / Defence80 / 85 / 7590 / 90 / 85
Ranged8594
Magic8094
Herblore7890
Hitpoints8595

Ask in #raids-lfg for a learner run. A mentor joins every Saturday run.

Gear

Bring one setup for each combat style. A blowpipe or a crossbow covers most ranged rooms. A trident does the job for the mage rooms until you get a better staff.

  • Melee: the best stab weapon you have, for Vasa and the guardians.
  • Ranged: a crossbow with ruby bolts (e), for the Great Olm.
  • Magic: a trident or better, for the mage hand.

Room order

  1. Scout for a layout with Tekton or Vasa and no Vespula.
  2. Do the puzzle rooms while the team brews and restocks.
  3. Take the combat rooms in the order the layout gives.
  4. Prepare for the Great Olm with full supplies.

Do not leave during the Olm. The team cannot finish the raid one player short.
