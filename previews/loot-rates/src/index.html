Round 2: Foundry kill rates on the reference page (step 4)

Where staff set a kill rate for a source that OSRSClans gives none. The API is built: PUT and DELETE /reference/kill-rates/{source} need frenzy edit, and the loot rate routes serve the rate marked Foundry. The Hallowed Sepulchre sources are live and have no OSRSClans rate. The Grand Hallowed Coffin rate of 6 an hour and Hammer Time as the staff member who set it are mock. Each candidate also moves the page onto the OSRSClans routes (step 5).
