# THE FIVE UNIVERSAL AXES (the compression)

1. **Existence** - What entities are present?
2. **Arrangement** - How are they organised? (structure, space)
3. **Dynamics** - How do they change? (time, evolution)
4. **Interaction** - How do they influence one another? (relations, behaviour, causality)
5. **Abstraction** - What meaning or principle can be inferred? (semantics, function, prediction)

_Statistical, sequential, fractal, causal, semantic and behavioural are specialised perspectives on one or more of these five._

# PATTERN WORK

Representation kinds: symbolic, numerical, geometric, topological, information-theoretic, probabilistic, dynamical, computational.

Operations on a pattern: detection, classification, explanation, prediction, generation.

## Five foundational questions

1. **Existence** - what objects are possible? Numbers, sets, spaces, functions, structures.
2. **Structure** - how are objects organised? Symmetry, algebraic laws, graph connectivity, geometric arrangement.
3. **Transformation** - how can objects change while preserving rules? Functions, operators, maps, algorithms.
4. **Uncertainty and possibility** - what states can occur, and how likely? Random variables, probability spaces, statistical models.
5. **Abstraction** - what general principle sits beneath the cases? Group theory from symmetry, category theory from relationships, algorithms from procedures.
